Rules: one post per group, never the same day in several groups; read and reply to others for a week first; the post is in the owner's first person, no selling, with one question at the end; each post goes with exactly one screenshot of the program (file name in `slike/izvor/`). ## Version 1 — “I built it for myself” (image: snimak-33-po-meri.png)

I'm a cabinet maker from Belgrade; I build kitchens and wardrobes from melamine chipboard. For years I wrote my cut lists by hand, and at least once a month a panel was missing or 18 mm short. Last year I sat down and started building the program I needed: I draw the room, line the cabinets up along the wall, and it gives me the panels with edge banding, the hardware and the price. The cut list is always current; I copy it and send it to the panel saw. It's called TOPICA, the trial is free and it works offline. I'm not after anything, I'm honestly curious: how do you do your cut lists today — by hand, Excel, some program?

## Version 2 — “one cabinet, eight panels” (image: snimak-12-korpus-sa-vratima.png)

A 600 × 720 × 560 base cabinet: two sides 702 × 539, bottom 600 × 539, shelf 562 × 519, two rails 564 × 100, front 717 × 597 and a hardboard back. Eight panels, and that's a single unit. When the customer changes the width from 600 to 450, you work it all out again. So I built a program where you set the cabinet against the wall and the sizes, edging and hardware recalculate themselves. A cabinet maker built it, so there's nothing in it you don't need. Question for you: which cut list mistake do you most often catch only at installation?

## Answers to the most common comments

**“How much is it?”** — The trial is free, no account, no card. I haven't published the price of the full version yet; it will be fair for a one-man shop, not for a factory. Whoever leaves an e-mail on the website hears first.

**“Does it run on Mac / phone?”** — No, Windows 10 and 11 only. Mac and phone aren't planned for now; honestly, most workshops I know run on a Windows laptop.

**“How is it better than…?”** — I'd rather not compare other people's software, each has its own strengths. Mine is built to be simple: room, cabinets along the wall, cut list and price, no training. If you need CNC export or sheet nesting, that isn't there yet, and I say so up front.

**“Does it do a cut list?”** — It does, that's the whole point: per cabinet and per material, with edge banding and quantities, plus a “By size” view where identical sizes sit on one line, so you copy it and send it to the panel saw or print it to PDF.

**“Is it available in German / Croatian?”** — The program is in Serbian and English today. The website exists in several languages, and the program gets translated once enough people ask for a language; tell me which one you need.
