**Categories/tags:** cabinet design, kitchen design, cut list, furniture design, woodworking, CAD
**Description:**
TOPICA is made by a cabinet maker, for cabinet makers who build custom panel furniture (kitchens, fitted wardrobes, cabinets) from 18 mm chipboard. You enter the room, line up base, wall, tall, sink, oven and corner cabinets along the wall, and the program knows the construction: frameless carcass, System 32, edge banding, 35 mm cup hinges, drawers, plinth, worktop and end panels. It instantly gives you 3D with real decors, a millimetre-exact cut list grouped by material, a “By size” view ready to copy to the panel saw, a hardware list and a price from your own workshop prices. No account, no internet, one project file. Currently in Serbian and English. No CNC export or sheet nesting in this version.
