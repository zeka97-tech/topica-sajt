**Name:** TOPICA
**Tagline (≤ 60):** Cabinet design software made by a cabinet maker
**Topics:** Design Tools, Productivity, Windows
**Links:** https://zeka97-tech.github.io/topica-sajt/en/
**Gallery:** 3–5 slika 1270×760 (izvoz iz snimaka 1366×768 bez uvećanja: iseći), prva = kuhinja u 3D (41), druga = korpus koji se rastavlja (snimak sa sajta, odeljak „Jedan korpus“), treća = krojna lista (33); video: YouTube snimak od 3 minuta.
**Description:**
I'm a cabinet maker from Belgrade. For years I wrote cut lists by hand and once a month a panel was missing or 18 mm short. So I built the program I needed. You draw the room, line up cabinets along the wall, and TOPICA knows the construction (European standard, System 32, edge banding, hinges, drawers, plinth, worktop). It instantly gives you 3D with real decors, a millimetre-exact cut list grouped by material, a “By size” view you copy and send to the saw, hardware and a price from your own prices. Windows, offline, no account. Free trial; price for the full version coming soon.

**First (maker) comment:**
Hi everyone — cabinet maker here, not a software company. TOPICA does one thing: gets a workshop from the room to the cut list without mistakes. What it does NOT do yet: CNC export, sheet nesting, Mac. It's in Serbian and English today. I'd love to hear from anyone who builds panel furniture: what does your cut list look like and where do mistakes usually happen? I'll answer every comment.
