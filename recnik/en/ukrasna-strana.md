The end panel (decorative side) is an 18 mm board in the front decor that covers the visible side of the carcass at the end of a run. The [carcass](./korpus.html) is usually made of white or grey chipboard, so when the run doesn't go from wall to wall, the last side is left bare. The end panel hides it and the kitchen looks like one piece.

## Standard sizes

- Thickness **18 mm**, the same material and decor as the [front](./front.html).
- Height and depth follow the carcass: on a 720 × 560 base unit the panel covers the side and goes down to the floor or to the plinth, as agreed with the customer.
- Edging: visible edges with **1 mm** tape like the front, heavily used ones **2 mm**.

When the panel goes down to the floor, the [plinth](./cokla.html) ends against it, not under it.

## In the workshop

The panel is screwed to the side from inside the carcass, so the heads don't show, or glued. Before that the grain direction is checked: the pattern has to run like on the front next to it, upright. A common mistake is that the end panel is forgotten in the cut list because “it isn't part of the carcass”, so it gets cut afterwards from an offcut, often with the wrong grain. Another mistake: the panel as deep as the side (560), with the front sticking out 18 mm in front of it; that is why the panel is usually cut deeper by the front thickness, so it sits flush with the fronts.

## How TOPICA handles it

In the program the end panel is part of the carcass body: when you move or delete the carcass, the panel goes with it, and its sizes, edging and price go into the cut list like any other panel ([features](../#mogucnosti)).

One more panel in the list, and the kitchen looks as if it were made from one piece.
