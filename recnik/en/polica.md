A **shelf** is a horizontal panel inside the carcass that rests on four supports in the holes of the [side](./bok.html). Unlike the bottom and top, a shelf does not tie the box together: it can be taken out and moved in height. That is why its sizes differ from those of the bottom.

## Standard sizes

- Chipboard **18 mm**.
- Width: the internal carcass width less the clearance for the supports. In the program's example for a 600 unit the shelf is **562 × 519 mm**, i.e. 2 mm narrower than the internal 564 and **set back** from the front edge of the side.
- Edging: **0.45 mm** on the front edge.
- Shelf supports: to [System 32](../blog/sistem-32.html), Ø5 mm holes at **32 mm** centres, the first row at **37 mm** from the front edge of the side (centre line); the same row of holes serves the hinges and runners too, as in the hardware manufacturer's catalog.
- Wide shelves sag under load; the usual answer is to split the unit with a [divider](./pregrada.html) or make the shelf thicker.

## In the workshop

The support holes are drilled to the System 32 jig before assembly, in both sides from the same edge, so the shelves don't sit crooked. The shelf is cut a little shorter than the inside size so it comes out without catching, and set back from the front edge so it isn't seen behind the door gap. Common mistake: holes drilled from different edges on the left and right side, so every shelf sits askew; another is a shelf at the full inside size that jams and scratches the edging.

![A carcass with an open door in TOPICA: one shelf at mid height, dimensions to the wall and from the floor](../slike/web/snimak-52-ruka-jedna-vrata-1600.webp "The shelf is set back from the front edge and shorter than the inside width so it can be taken out.")

## How TOPICA handles it

The program keeps one setback for all shelves in the project and works out the width from the clearance itself; you change the number of shelves in the carcass, and the sizes go straight into the [cut list](../blog/krojna-lista.html) ([features](../#mogucnosti)).

The shelf is the only panel in the carcass that is meant to move, so cut it so that it can.
