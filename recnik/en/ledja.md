The **back** is the thin board that closes the carcass at the rear. It is not decoration: the back keeps the box square, and without it the carcass “walks” into a parallelogram. It is made of 3 mm hardboard (HDF), white, coloured or in the carcass decor.

## Standard sizes

- **Hardboard (HDF) 3 mm**; in the cut list “no grain”, because the pattern doesn't matter.
- Two ways of fitting: in a **10 mm deep rebate** routed into the sides, bottom and top, or **edge to edge on the outside**, nailed to the rear edge of the carcass (the TOPICA default).
- The edge-to-edge size for a 600 × 720 carcass in the program's example: **717 × 597 mm**. In a rebate the size is the inside size + 2 × 10 mm, less a small clearance so it goes in without force.
- The carcass depth is worked out with the back: 560 = 539 + front 18 + **back 3**.

## In the workshop

An edge-to-edge back is cut last, against the assembled carcass: you lay the hardboard on, line up one corner and that way check the box is square before nailing or stapling it. The rebate is the neater option for visible wall units and wardrobes, but it needs routing before assembly and a more exact size. Common mistake: the back cut to the drawing while the carcass was assembled a millimetre out of square, so the hardboard sticks out on one side; another is a forgotten opening for the waste and power on a [sink unit](../blog/standardne-mere-kuhinje.html) or oven housing.

## How TOPICA handles it

The program assumes an edge-to-edge back on the outside and writes it in the [cut list](../blog/krojna-lista.html) as 3 mm hardboard, no grain; you change the thickness and the method in the workshop settings for the whole project ([features](../#mogucnosti)). Coloured hardboard follows the [carcass](./korpus.html) decor.

Three millimetres of hardboard are what keep eighteen millimetres of chipboard in line.
