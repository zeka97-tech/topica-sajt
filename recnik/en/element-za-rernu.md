The oven housing is a base kitchen carcass with a recess that a built-in oven goes into; above it there is usually a hob in the worktop, below it a drawer. The recess is an opening without a front, to exactly the sizes the appliance instructions ask for.

## Standard sizes

- Recess width **at least 568 mm**; the unit is therefore usually **600 mm** wide. The program warns when the unit width does not give a 568 recess.
- Recess height **≈ 590–600 mm**, depth **550 mm**; exact sizes from the oven manufacturer's instructions.
- Below the recess there is a drawer; above it the hob, with a cut-out in the [worktop](./radna-ploca.html) to the manufacturer's template.
- The rest of the [carcass](./korpus.html) as on other base units: height 720, depth 560, plinth 100.

## In the workshop

The oven stands on the floor of the recess and is screwed through its own frame to the sides; the ventilation space behind and below is left as the instructions say, without closing it with hardboard where the appliance needs an opening. Common mistake: a 560 recess “like the carcass depth”, without checking the instructions, so the oven sticks out or won't go in until the back is cut. Another: the drawer under the oven with a box that hits the recess floor; a drawer box never goes through the panel above. A third: a 600 unit with 18 mm sides has a clear opening of 564, so a 568 recess does not fit without changing the construction; that is why the recess size is checked before cutting.

## How TOPICA handles it

In the program the oven and hob are standard recesses without a brand, to the specification; when the width does not give 568 mm the program warns you, and the sizes of the recess, the drawer below and the cut-out go into the cut list ([features](../#mogucnosti)).

The recess is sized to the appliance, not the appliance to the recess.
