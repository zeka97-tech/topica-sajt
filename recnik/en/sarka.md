A **hinge** is the hardware that holds the door on the carcass side and lets it open. In panel furniture that is the concealed (cup) hinge: a **Ø35 mm cup** sunk into the front and a mounting plate screwed to the side. The hinge type decides how far the [front](./front.html) covers the side, so it is chosen before the front is cut, not after.

## Standard sizes

- Three types by overlay: **full overlay** (18 mm overlay, the front covers the whole side), **half overlay** (9 mm, two fronts share one divider), **inset** (0 mm, the front sits between the sides).
- Cup Ø35, drilling depth **12.5–13 mm** in an 18 mm front; cup centre **21.5–22.5 mm** from the front edge (distance K = 3–6 mm), to the hardware manufacturer's catalog.
- The mounting plate on the side to [System 32](../blog/sistem-32.html), the first row of holes at 37 mm from the front edge.
- Number of hinges by front height (program default): **up to 900 mm: 2 · up to 1600: 3 · up to 2000: 4 · taller: 5**. The end cups at **100 mm** from the top and bottom edge, the rest evenly spaced.
- Chosen by overlay and number, without a manufacturer; GTV is the default, Blum/Hettich/Grass per project.

## In the workshop

The cups are drilled first, the hinge is pressed into the front and only then, with the door held in place, is the mounting plate marked on the side. After hanging, the hinge is adjusted in three directions until the 3 mm gap is the same top and bottom. Common mistake: the cup centre taken from the edge differently for each door, so the gaps in the run are not the same; another is a hinge behind the [rail](./precka.html), so the door can neither be taken off nor adjusted.

## How TOPICA handles it

You choose the hinge type and the opening side, and the program sets the number of cups by front height itself, their position and the front size by overlay; a hinge never lands behind a rail ([features](../#mogucnosti)).

A hinge is chosen with a pencil before cutting, not with a screwdriver after.
