The **carcass** is the box of the unit: all the panels that carry the furniture, without the front. It is made up of two [sides](./bok.html), the [bottom](./dno.html), the [top](./krov.html) on wall units or two [rails](./precka.html) on base units, the [back](./ledja.html) and the [shelves](./polica.html). The front hangs on the carcass, the worktop lies on it, and the sizes of everything else start from it.

## Standard sizes

- Material: melamine chipboard **18 mm** (variants 16, 19, 25 mm).
- Base unit: carcass **720** high, **560** deep (600 with the worktop), on **100 mm** legs.
- Wall unit: height **720** (variants 360, 900/920), depth **300–350**.
- Widths 150–1200 mm; the usual ones 300, 400, 450, 500, 600, 800, 900, 1000, 1200.
- Depth of the box itself = overall depth − front 18 − back 3. For 560 that is **539 mm**.

Example of a 600 × 720 × 560 base carcass: side 702 × 539 ×2, bottom 600 × 539, shelf 562 × 519, rails 564 × 100 ×2, front 717 × 597, back 717 × 597 in 3 mm hardboard. The full list is in the guide on the [cut list](../blog/krojna-lista.html).

## In the workshop

First decide who sits over whom: bottom over the sides (sides 18 shorter) or sides over the bottom. Every size depends on that decision, so it is not changed mid-job. It is assembled with cam fittings or confirmat screws, the back is tacked into the rebate or nailed on the outside, and only then is the front hung. Common mistake: a side cut at 720 instead of 702 when the bottom sits over it, so the carcass is 18 mm taller and the worktop doesn't line up with the neighbour.

## How TOPICA handles it

You pick the carcass from the catalog, enter width, height and depth, and the program derives every panel, the edging and the hardware; what you see in 3D is what goes into the cut list ([how it works](../#kako-radi)). Bottom over sides is the default, so the sides shorten themselves by 18 mm.

A carcass is simple once you have agreed with yourself which panel sits over which.
