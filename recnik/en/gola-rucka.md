A Gola profile is an aluminium profile shaped like a C or an L, built into the carcass instead of a handle: your fingers go into the groove of the profile and pull the front. The front stays smooth, and the kitchen gets a horizontal line along the whole run.

## Standard sizes

- **C** profile (for one row of fronts, usually directly under the worktop) or **L** profile (between two rows, e.g. drawer above drawer); profile sizes to the manufacturer's catalog.
- Fronts are cut **shorter or narrower** by the profile; the [carcass](./korpus.html) side gets a notch for the profile to sit in.
- The sink unit loses its front rail, because the profile takes its place.
- The gap at the profile is not the usual 3 mm but whatever the profile gives — see [front gap](./zazor-fronta.html).

## In the workshop

The profile is cut to the length of the run and screwed to the sides in the notch, and only then are the fronts hung; that is why the notch in the side is made before assembling the carcass, to the drawing from the manufacturer's data sheet. Common mistake: fronts cut to the normal size, then trimmed by the profile height afterwards, losing the edging on one edge. Another: the profile run through the side of the neighbouring carcass instead of each carcass having its own notch; the profile never goes through a panel. A third: a profile in a colour that doesn't match the front edging, so the line breaks at every unit.

## How TOPICA handles it

You enter the profile and its sizes from the manufacturer's sheet in one place, and the program shortens the fronts, adds the notch to the side and removes the front rail of the sink unit ([features](../#mogucnosti)).

Without handles there is less hardware, but more measuring.
