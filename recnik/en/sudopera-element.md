The sink unit is the base kitchen carcass the sink is fitted into: it has no back rail, so there is room for the trap and the waste, while the front rail stays to hold the sides and carry the worktop. The sink cut-out is made in the [worktop](./radna-ploca.html), not in the carcass.

## Standard sizes

- Widths **600, 800, 900 and 1000 mm**; carcass height 720 and depth 560 as on the other base units.
- The front rail, **100 mm** high, stays; the back one is left out.
- The cut-out in the worktop **to the sink manufacturer's template**; the 600 worktop depth and the 20–30 mm overhang are the same as on the rest of the run.
- With a [Gola profile](./gola-rucka.html) the unit loses the front rail too, because the profile takes its place.

## In the workshop

The carcass is assembled like any other, only with one rail; the bottom is under the trap, so it is often protected with foil or made from moisture-resistant board. The front is usually a pair of doors, or a fixed dummy drawer front at the top and doors below. Common mistake: the worktop cut-out made to the outside size of the sink, so the sink falls through the worktop; the manufacturer's template gives the cut-out size, not the sink size. Another: a back rail out of habit, so the trap doesn't fit and the rail gets cut on site. A third: a dishwasher next to the sink with no plinth in front of it, so the plinth line is broken.

## How TOPICA handles it

The sink unit in the program's catalog comes without a back rail and with its own drawers, and the [plinth](./cokla.html) in front of the sink and the dishwasher runs in one line with the rest of the run ([how it works](../#kako-radi)).

One rail less, one template more — and the sink drops in first time.
