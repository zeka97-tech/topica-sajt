A **drawer** is the moving box inside the carcass, and the **runner** is the hardware it slides on. A drawer consists of the drawer front ([front](./front.html)), the box (two sides, a back and a bottom) and a pair of runners on the carcass sides. A drawer without a box does not exist: a front on its own is just a front.

## Standard sizes

- **Wooden box on ball-bearing telescopic runners**: clearance **13 mm per side** (internal carcass width − 26 = outside width of the box), runner lengths 250–550 in 50 mm steps, box sides 18 mm, bottom 3 mm hardboard (3 mm narrower and shorter than the box), side heights M 85 / K 120 / C 150 / F 180.
- Roller runners: clearance **12.5 mm**, lengths 250–500, heights 85/120/150.
- **Metal box** (GTV Modern Box, the program default): bottom = clear opening − 86 in width and NL − 24 in length, back = opening − 97, heights 68/83/146/210 mm; Modern Box PRO has its own deductions (−75 / −24 / −87). All to the manufacturer's catalog.
- Runner = the longest nominal length that fits the carcass depth: a carcass 539 deep takes a **500** runner.
- Drawer fronts: **3 mm** gap between neighbours, 1 mm edging.

## In the workshop

The runner is chosen first, and the box is cut to suit it; the other way round does not work. The runners are drilled to [System 32](../blog/sistem-32.html) on both sides from the same edge, then the front is hung and adjusted to the gap with its neighbours. Common mistake: the top drawer box cut without allowing for the [rail](./precka.html) above, so it hits it; another is the 13 mm clearance taken on one side only, so the box won't go in. The full calculation is in the guide [Drawers: runner and box](../blog/fioke-vodjica-i-sanduk.html).

## How TOPICA handles it

You choose the runner type and the number of drawers, and the program derives the box, bottom and front to the catalog, takes the longest runner that fits and makes sure the box never passes through the panel above ([features](../#mogucnosti)).

A drawer starts with the runner; the front is the last thing you hang.
