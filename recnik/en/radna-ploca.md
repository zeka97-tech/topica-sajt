The **worktop** is the thick top that lies across all the base units and forms the working surface of the kitchen. Most often it is laminate on chipboard with a rounded front edge (postformed), less often solid wood, stone or compact laminate; the sizes and rules are the same. The worktop rests on the carcass [rails](./precka.html) and is screwed from below.

## Standard sizes

- Thickness **38 mm** (variants 28 and 40).
- Depth **600 mm** (620/635 for deeper units): carcass 560 + front 18 + overhang **~20–30 mm** past the front. At the side 20–30 mm of overhang where there is no wall.
- Height of the top edge from the floor ≈ **858 mm** (720 carcass + 100 legs + 38 worktop); in practice 850–920, to suit the user's height.
- Distance to the wall units: the bottom edge of the wall units at **1400 mm from the floor** (program default), so ≈ 500–550 mm above the worktop.
- Joint mitred or with an aluminium joining strip; the cut-outs for the sink and hob **to the manufacturer's template**, never by eye.
- Edging: on worktops without a rounded edge **2 mm** ABS on the front edge and the ends.

## In the workshop

The worktop is measured against the installed and levelled carcasses, not the drawing, because the wall rarely runs straight: the rear edge is scribed to the wall first, then the front. Cut-outs are cut from the underside, with masking tape, and sealed with silicone straight away, especially around the sink. Common mistake: a worktop cut to the sum of the unit widths without the side overhang, so the end of the run is left bare; another is a sink cut-out over a rail or a [side](./bok.html), because nobody looked at what was underneath.

## How TOPICA handles it

You build the worktop over the selected carcasses; the program gives it the depth, thickness and overhang from the settings and moves it with the carcasses when you move them, and the sink and hob are standard recesses to the [standard sizes](../blog/standardne-mere-kuhinje.html) ([features](../#mogucnosti)).

The worktop is the one board in the kitchen the customer touches every day, so measure it on site.
