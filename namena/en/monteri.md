More and more tradesmen work without a panel saw of their own: they measure at the customer's, order the cutting and edging from a service, then assemble and install. There the cut list is the only piece of paper that goes from your hands into someone else's. If it is right, the service doesn't call you; if it isn't, the mistake shows only on installation. TOPICA makes that list from the design, not from your notebook.

## A list the service understands

The “By size” view puts identical sizes on one line, grouped by material and thickness, with the [edging](../recnik/kant.html) mark and the quantity, exactly as it is cut: white 18 mm chipboard first, then fronts, then hardboard. The first dimension is the grain direction, so fronts are written upright and hardboard carries the “no grain” mark; the service doesn't have to guess which way the pattern runs. Sizes are net, after edging, which is the program default and worth telling the service in your first message. You copy the list with one click and send it as a message or e-mail, or print it to PDF. What every line means is in the glossary under [cut list](../recnik/krojna-lista.html).

## Example: what goes in the message

A 600 × 720 × 560 base carcass with a door and a shelf, eight panels:

| Panel | Size (mm) | Thk. | Edging | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Side | 702 × 539 | 18 | 1 long | 2 |
| Bottom | 600 × 539 | 18 | 1 long | 1 |
| Shelf | 562 × 519 | 18 | 1 long | 1 |
| Rail | 564 × 100 | 18 | 2 long | 2 |
| Front | 717 × 597 | 18 | 2 long, 2 short | 1 |
| Back (hardboard) | 717 × 597 | 3 | none | 1 |

A twelve-unit kitchen is a hundred or so lines like these. The program puts them together for all the carcasses at once, and alongside them gives the total board usage in m² and the edging length with 10 % waste, so you know how many sheets and how many metres of tape to order.

## Hardware and material before going to the shop

An installer buys the hardware himself and buys it once. Along with the panel list the program gives the hardware list: [hinges](../recnik/sarka.html) by front height (up to 900 mm two, up to 1600 three), [runners](../recnik/fioka-i-vodjica.html) as the longest nominal length that fits the carcass depth, legs four per unit up to 800 mm wide and six above, shelf supports to System 32. None of it is copied off a drawing.

## The project is one file

No account and no server: the project is a file on your computer. You send it to the colleague who cuts, take it on the laptop to the installation and open the 3D there with dimensions to the wall, to the neighbour and from the floor, and show the customer where everything goes before you drill the first hole. How the space is measured so the list is right is in the guide [how to measure a kitchen](../blog/kako-izmeriti-kuhinju.html); what else the program gives is in [features](../#mogucnosti).

## What the program doesn't do in this version

It doesn't make a CNC file or a cutting plan (nesting). The service does that on its own software and its own saw; what it needs is an exact list, and that is what you get. The whole way from the room to that list is [three steps](../#kako-radi).

Make your next list for the service in the program, then compare it with the one from the notebook.
