A chest of drawers, a TV unit, a bookcase, a bedside table: small jobs where a mistake pays least, because the earnings are small and there is no time to cut a panel twice. In the program these are the same [carcasses](../recnik/korpus.html) as kitchen ones, only with different sizes. You drop them into the room, enter width, height and depth, and the program derives the panels, drawers, hardware and price.

## A cabinet is a carcass with drawers

The usual chest of drawers is 400–450 mm deep and 800–900 high. The carcass has two sides, a bottom that sits over the sides (full width, sides 18 mm shorter), a top, a 3 mm hardboard back and fronts: drawers, doors or both. Edging is 0.45 mm on the visible carcass edges and 1 mm on the fronts, all four edges. You type the width, height and depth or drag the slider; the [dimensions](../#mogucnosti) to the wall, to the neighbour and from the floor are on screen all the time.

## Drawers to the catalog, not by eye

On a cabinet the drawers are the whole job. The program builds them to the catalog, not by estimate. For a wooden box on ball-bearing telescopic runners it allows a clearance of 13 mm per side, 18 mm box sides and a 3 mm hardboard bottom; the runners come in lengths of 250–550 in 50 mm steps. For a GTV Modern Box metal box the bottom is “clear opening − 86” wide and “NL − 24” long. The rules are strict: the runner is the longest nominal length that fits the carcass depth, the box never passes through the panel above, and [drawers](../recnik/fioka-i-vodjica.html) are counted from the top.

Example: a 900 × 850 × 450 cabinet with three drawers. The carcass depth is 450 − 18 (front) − 3 (back) = 429 mm, so the program takes a 400 runner. The clear opening is 900 − 36 = 864 wide, and from there come the box and bottom widths for the chosen system. Change the cabinet depth to 400 and the runner becomes 350, the box bottom shorter, the cut list new, without a single calculation on your part.

## Doors, shelves and System 32

The part of the cabinet with doors gets [hinges](../recnik/sarka.html) with a Ø35 cup: up to 900 mm of front height two, placed 100 mm from the top and bottom edge. Handles go per front, in one place for the whole project. Shelves in a bookcase sit on supports to System 32: a row of Ø5 holes at 32 mm centres, 37 mm from the front edge of the side, the same way the hardware manufacturers and CNC machines drill. The gap between neighbouring fronts is 3 mm, at the unit edge 1.5 mm.

## Several pieces, one project

A bedroom is a wardrobe, a chest of drawers and two bedside tables. In the program that is one file and one room: a cut list grouped by material for all the pieces together, one hardware list, one board usage and one price at your prices for boards, [edging](../recnik/kant.html), hardware and labour. How a quote for the customer is made from that is in the guide [how much a fitted kitchen costs](../blog/koliko-kosta-kuhinja-po-meri.html); the principle is the same for a cabinet.

A small piece, the same sum as a big one; the only difference is that the program does it in a minute.
